Q3 – Average Monthly Revenue by Region
sql
-- Q3. Determine the average monthly revenue generated by customers in each region.
SELECT c.region, ROUND(AVG(s.revenue)) AS "Average monthly revenue"
FROM customers c
INNER JOIN subscription s
ON c.customer_id = s.customer_id
GROUP BY c.region;
Business Question: What is the average monthly revenue generated by customers in each region?
Business Insight: SW and NW regions record the highest average monthly revenue, while SE and SS lag behind. This highlights regional differences in spending power.
Recommendation: Focus infrastructure and promotions in SW and NW, while designing growth strategies to boost weaker regions.

Q5 – Revenue by State
sql
-- Q5. Which states generate the highest total revenue?
SELECT c.state, ROUND(SUM(s.revenue)) AS "Total revenue"
FROM customers c
INNER JOIN subscription s
ON c.customer_id = s.customer_id
GROUP BY c.state
ORDER BY "Total revenue" DESC;
Business Question: Which states generate the highest total revenue?
Business Insight: Kaduna, Kano, and Lagos are the top revenue‑generating states. These states drive the bulk of company income.
Recommendation: Prioritize these states for marketing and network expansion, while investigating why other states underperform.

Bonus 5 – Regional Management Report
sql
-- Bonus 5. Produce a management report showing region, total customers, average age, total revenue, highest revenue, lowest revenue.
SELECT c.region,
       COUNT(*) AS total_customers,
       ROUND(AVG(c.age),2) AS average_age,
       SUM(s.revenue) AS total_revenue,
       MAX(s.revenue) AS highest_revenue,
       MIN(s.revenue) AS lowest_revenue
FROM customers c
INNER JOIN subscription s
ON c.customer_id = s.customer_id
GROUP BY c.region;
Business Question: What is the management report showing region, total customers, average age, total revenue, highest revenue, and lowest revenue?
Business Insight: NW leads in both customer count and revenue, while SW follows closely. NC has the smallest footprint. Average ages vary, with NW and SE skewing older.
Recommendation: Invest heavily in NW and SW, while targeting NC for customer acquisition. Tailor Premium promotions to older demographics in NW and SE.
